About Good Governance

Good governance refers to the responsible management of public affairs and resources. It promotes fairness, transparency, accountability, and active citizen participation in decision-making.

Core Principles

Transparency

Government actions and decisions should be open and accessible.

Accountability

Officials must be answerable for their actions and decisions.

Participation

Citizens should have opportunities to engage in governance.

Rule of Law

All individuals and institutions are subject to the law.

Benefits
